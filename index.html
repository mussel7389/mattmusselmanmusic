About

Hi! I’m Matt Musselman and I’m a trombonist, composer, and educator based in New York City.

My trombone playing has taken me from studies at The Juilliard School to performing with Feist at Radio City Music Hall to recording with Vince Giordano & The Nighthawks for the soundtrack to the HBO series Boardwalk Empire.

I’ve composed the music for animated shorts such as Urf Durfler: PI, arranged Great American Songbook repertoire for Broadway vocalist Euan Morton, orchestrated Kurt Weill compositions for Weimar cabaret artist Daniel Isengart, and transcribed Shanghai shidaiqu songs for regular performances at the Asian neo-noir nightclub The Red Pavilion.

As a former NYC DOE special education teacher, I bring extensive pedagogical experience, knowledge, and passion into any musical learning environment.

I’m always interested in new musical adventures and I would love to collaborate with you! Email me at: matt@mattmusselmanmusic.com

Composer

I’ve composed the music for animated shorts such as Urf Durfler: PI, arranged Great American Songbook repertoire for Broadway vocalist Euan Morton, orchestrated Kurt Weill compositions for Weimar cabaret artist Daniel Isengart, and transcribed Shanghai shidaiqu songs for regular performances at the Asian neo-noir nightclub The Red Pavilion.

As a music copyist, I have a vast skill set. I’m able to:

  • use all of the major music notation programs (Sibelius, Dorico, Muse Score, Finale)
  • prepare clean, professional looking scores and parts for performance, recording, or publication
  • transcribe complex music from audio with extreme accuracy
  • proofread and edit existing scores and parts
  • arrange or orchestrate existing music for any instrumentation
  • “migrate” music from one program to another (i.e. Finale to Dorico)
  • organize and manage sheet music files for a project using Google Drive
  • meet tight deadlines for urgent projects
  • communicate regularly regarding changes and revisions as the needs of projects evolve
